Source: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5WC95

Citation: Kaya, H., Tüfekci, P., & Uzun, E. (2019). Predicting CO and NOx emissions from gas turbines: novel data and
a benchmark PEMS. Turkish Journal of Electrical Engineering & Computer Sciences, 27(6), 4783-4796.
https://doi.org/10.3906/elk-1807-87

The data was collected from 2011-2015, there are 36733 hourly rows of 11 columns (9 sensor input measurements & 2 sensor output measurements, CO & NOx) aggregated over one hour (by means of average or sum) from a gas turbine located in Turkey's north western region. Data is chronological but not every hour is recorded.
